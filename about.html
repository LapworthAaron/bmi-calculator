Our mission

get-bmi.co.uk was created to provide a fast, accurate, and completely free BMI calculator that works for both adults and children. We believe everyone should have easy access to basic health screening tools without needing to sign up, download an app, or hand over personal data.

What makes this calculator different

  • Adults and children — Unlike most calculators, we support children aged 2–19 using official CDC growth chart percentile data, as well as standard adult BMI categories.
  • Privacy-first — All calculations happen locally in your browser. Your health data is never sent to a server, stored, or shared.
  • Comprehensive results — Beyond basic BMI, we provide your BMI category, BMI Prime, Ponderal Index, healthy weight range, and (for children) CDC percentile ranking.
  • Evidence-based — Our categories, thresholds, and guidance are based on established standards from the WHO, NHS, and CDC.

Our data sources

We take accuracy seriously. Our calculator uses the following established references:

  • World Health Organisation (WHO) — Adult BMI classification standards and global obesity guidelines.
  • National Health Service (NHS) — UK-specific BMI guidance, including ethnicity-adjusted thresholds for South Asian, Chinese, and Black African populations.
  • Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) — BMI-for-age growth charts and LMS parameters for children and teens aged 2–19.

Medical disclaimer

This tool is for informational purposes only and does not constitute medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. BMI is a screening measure, not a diagnostic tool. It has well-documented limitations — it does not account for muscle mass, bone density, body fat distribution, age, or ethnicity-specific risk factors.

Always consult a qualified healthcare professional (such as your GP) for personalised medical advice about your weight and health.
