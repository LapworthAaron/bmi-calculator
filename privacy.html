Privacy Policy

How we handle your data on get-bmi.co.uk

Last updated: March 2026

Who we are

This website, get-bmi.co.uk, provides a free Body Mass Index (BMI) calculator for adults and children. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how we use data.

What data we collect

Our BMI calculator runs entirely in your browser. The health data you enter (age, height, weight, sex) is never transmitted to our servers, never stored, and never shared with any third party. All calculations happen locally on your device.

We do not require you to create an account, log in, or provide any personal information to use this tool.

Cookies and advertising

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements on this site. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. These cookies allow Google to display personalised or non-personalised advertisements depending on your preferences.

  • Google uses cookies such as the DART cookie to serve ads based on your browsing behaviour.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also opt out of third-party vendor cookies by visiting aboutads.info.

Google Fonts

We use Google Fonts to display the Inter typeface. When you load a page, your browser connects to Google servers to download the font files. Google may log your IP address in accordance with their privacy policy. No other data is shared with Google through this service.

Analytics

We do not currently use any analytics services. If we add analytics in the future, this policy will be updated accordingly.

Third-party links

Our site contains links to external resources including NHS, WHO, and CDC websites. These third-party sites have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their content or practices.

Children's privacy

Our BMI calculator includes a mode for children aged 2–19 using CDC growth chart data. No personal data is collected, stored, or transmitted for any user, including children. All calculations are performed locally in the browser.

Your rights under UK GDPR

Under the UK General Data Protection Regulation, you have the right to:

  • Access any personal data we hold about you (we hold none)
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to data processing
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO)

Since we do not collect or store any personal data, there is nothing to access, correct, or delete. However, you may manage your cookie preferences at any time through your browser settings.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us.

This tool is for informational purposes only and does not constitute medical advice.

Information based on WHO, NHS, and CDC guidelines. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional.
